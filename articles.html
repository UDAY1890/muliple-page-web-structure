The Importance of Clean HTML

Published on August 12, 2026 by Web Dev Team

In the modern web ecosystem, it is tempting to jump straight into complex styling and interactive JavaScript. However, the true foundation of any great website lies in its underlying HTML structure.

Accessibility Matters

When you use the correct tags—like navigation menus in a <nav> and main content in a <main> tag—you are actively making the internet a better place for users who rely on assistive technologies.

"Good design begins with good structure. Code for people first, and browsers second."

Better SEO Results

Search engine crawlers aren't humans; they are robots reading your text. By wrapping a list of features in an unordered list, or defining your headers hierarchically, you give those robots a perfect map of what your content actually means.
